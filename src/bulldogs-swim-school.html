Bulldogs Swim School Lessons

Bulldogs Swim School was created out of a passion for seeing children have fun while learning valuable skills in the water. Since opening in June 2022, we have helped many students build foundational skills and move forward with confidence. Lessons are year-round, for ages 3–12 of every swimming background.

Level 1: Alanos

Ages 3–5. Comfort in the water, floating, rolling over and water safety.

Level 2: Continentals

Ages 4–6. Kicking, arm strokes and side breathing, without assistance.

Level 3: Mammuts

Ages 5–7. Freestyle, bilateral breathing, backstroke and elementary backstroke.

Level 4: Mastiffs

Ages 6–8. All the basic strokes, then on to the Club's development groups.

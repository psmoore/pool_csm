One calendar, many pages. Each calendar on this site is a different view of the same schedule. The Masters page shows only Masters, the Swim School page shows only lessons, and this page shows everything. When the aquatics office changes a booking once, every page shows the change:
  • No PDFs to replace. The Fall sheet replaced the June sheet at a new address, so every saved link and printout still shows June.
  • Hours, not just counts. The sheet says "3" for Monday at 10 AM. The calendar says which three lanes, and what the other seventeen are for.
  • Subscriptions. Members can follow a program and be emailed its changes, or add it to Google, Apple or Outlook calendars.
  • Reservations stay in Empower M.E. The calendar shows when lanes are open; members still reserve them in the portal.

How this calendar was built

From the College's own published sources: the Fall lane availability sheet (effective 9/11/2026), the Masters practice schedule on its second page, the Bulldog Swim Club and Swim School schedules, and the water polo notice on the entrance. Where a source gives a number of lanes but not which ones, the lanes shown are our estimate. Lanes the sheet marks as unavailable with no program named are shown as Closed / Other Programming.

About Bulldog Swim Club

Bulldog Swim Club offers competitive and recreational swim teams for ages 5–18. Emphasis is on stroke technique and on developing swimmers who want to improve their swimming and racing. Our developmental, non-competitive groups foster a love for the water while building the skills to move up to the next level. Practices are held rain or shine; athletes leave the pool if there is lightning.

Non-competitive groups

Blue II and Varsity: developmental training without required meets.

Competitive groups

Bronze, Silver, Silver Advanced and Gold. All require USA Swimming registration, practice attendance, intrasquad meets and coach approval.

Practices this week

Live

Every Bulldog practice, labeled with the groups in the water. Tap a group in the filter row to see only your swimmer's group, or Subscribe to put it on the family calendar.

The Club's schedule names the groups practicing at the same time but not how they split the lanes, so each block carries all of them together, on lanes that are our estimate.

Why a parent would rather have this than a schedule image.
  • One group at a time. A Silver family taps Silver and sees only Silver's practices.
  • Changes reach families. When a practice moves for a meet or a pool closure, families following the group are emailed. On 9/30 and 10/28 collegiate water polo holds the Olympic Pool until 6:15 PM, a fact that today lives only on a paper sign at the door.
  • On their phones. Subscribe adds the group's practices to Google, Apple or Outlook calendars and keeps them current.
